How to download YouTube videos on Android

Save a YouTube video to your Android gallery with no app. Use Chrome, paste the link and download the MP4.

Steps for Android

  1. In the YouTube app tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open Chrome and go to clipharbor.epsingh.in.
  3. Paste the link and press Get download links.
  4. Tap 720p or 1080p. Allow the download if Chrome asks.

Find it in your gallery

Open Files by Google, go to Downloads and your video is there. Most phones add it to Gallery or Google Photos automatically. If not, move it to the DCIM or Movies folder.

Save on storage

Pick 480p or 720p for long videos. A 10 minute video is roughly 60 MB at 720p.

iPhone users

Use Safari on iOS 13 or later. After the download opens in Safari, tap the download icon, then Files, and save to Photos from the share sheet.
