YouTube to MP3 converter

Convert a YouTube video to MP3 online. Free, no app, no sign up. Works on mobile and desktop.

  1. Paste the video link on ClipHarbor.
  2. Press Get download links.
  3. Tap MP3 audio. The file is converted on our server and downloaded to your device.

Audio quality

Files are encoded at 192 kbps, which is clear for music, podcasts, lectures and interviews while keeping the file small.

Best for

Lectures you want to hear offline, podcasts, your own music uploads and royalty free tracks.

Tips

If the file seems quiet, raise the volume in your player. Long videos take a little more time to convert, so keep the tab open until the download begins.

FAQs

How to download YouTube videos on a laptop without any software?

Paste the video link into the box at the top of this page, press Get download links and pick a quality. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install on Windows, Mac or Linux.

How to download a YouTube video in Chrome?

Open ClipHarbor in Chrome, paste the link and choose MP4 or MP3. Chrome saves the file to your Downloads folder. No extension is needed.

How to download a YouTube video on PC for free?

ClipHarbor is free with no login, no limit and no watermark. Copy the link from the address bar, paste it here and download.

How to download a YouTube video to mobile gallery on Android?

Open ClipHarbor in Chrome on your phone, paste the link and download the MP4. Then open the Files app, go to Downloads and the video appears in your Gallery or Photos app shortly.
