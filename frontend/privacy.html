Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

What we collect

We do not ask for accounts, names or emails. The video link you submit is processed to prepare your download and is not stored after the file is delivered.

Temporary files

Converted files are kept on our server only while your download runs and are deleted automatically, with a safety purge within 15 minutes.

Logs and cookies

Our host may keep standard server logs such as IP address and time for security. We do not use tracking cookies for downloads. If advertising or analytics are added later, this page will be updated.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Video data is fetched from YouTube.
