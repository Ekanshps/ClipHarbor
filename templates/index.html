GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I download any YouTube video?

Only download videos you own, have permission to use, or are otherwise legally permitted to download. Platform terms and local law apply.

Which video resolutions are available?

Options depend on the source video and the formats exposed by yt-dlp. The app cannot create a resolution that the source does not provide.

Why did MP3 conversion fail?

MP3 conversion requires FFmpeg to be installed on the server. Some source formats may also be temporarily unavailable.

Is there really no rate limit?

The app does not impose a user-account quota in this starter version, but YouTube, your server, and your hosting provider may impose their own limits. This tool does not attempt to evade them.

Does this tool add a watermark?

The app does not add a watermark to the media. Source content and any marks already present in it remain unchanged.
