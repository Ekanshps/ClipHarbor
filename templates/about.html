{{ site_name }} is a lightweight interface for preparing authorized YouTube media downloads in available formats.

What it does

Paste a supported YouTube video URL, inspect available details, select MP4 video or MP3 audio, and prepare a file. The filename begins with a site-specific prefix and includes the video title.

What it does not do

This project does not bypass DRM, sign-in requirements, paywalls, or access controls. It does not guarantee unlimited access, a specific resolution, or uninterrupted availability.

Responsible use

Use the service only for content you own or have permission to download, and follow applicable laws and platform terms. The site is independent and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
